# Highbeam

> Highbeam is a business banking and finance platform built for modern consumer brands (D2C and e-commerce). It combines operating and high-yield accounts, a charge card with cashback on advertising spend, working capital and credit lines, bill pay, spend intelligence, and an applied-AI finance assistant (Luma). Banking services are provided through partner banks (Thread Bank, member FDIC) and Airwallex. Highbeam does not publish a public developer API, developer portal, or machine-readable API specification as of this writing.
